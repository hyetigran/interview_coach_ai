# Interview coaching

Language for reviewing a candidate's past interviews and preparing future answers.

## Language

**Hiring interview**:
An interview conducted by an employer to assess a candidate for an actual job opening.
_Avoid_: Real interview, which can imply a mock interview is not a genuine conversation.

**Mock interview**:
An interview conducted for practice rather than a hiring decision. It can be reviewed through the same coaching workflow as a hiring interview.

**Interview recording**:
An audio or video record of a past interview that the candidate provides for review, such as a recording from Google Meet or Zoom.
_Avoid_: Transcript when referring to the recording itself.

**Transcript**:
A written representation of what participants said during an interview, whether supplied directly or transcribed from a recording.
_Avoid_: Recording when referring only to the written text.

**Review**:
A candidate's saved workspace for examining one interview, its evidence, coaching, and preparation work.
_Avoid_: Interview when referring to the workspace rather than the conversation.

**Question thread**:
An interview question, the candidate's response, and related follow-up questions and responses considered together.
_Avoid_: Answer when referring to the entire question-and-follow-up discussion.

**Speaker confirmation**:
The candidate's identification of which recorded voice or voices belong to them.
_Avoid_: Transcript approval, which implies confirming every word.

**Transcript correction**:
A change intended to make the written account accurately reflect the recording, rather than improve what the candidate said.
_Avoid_: Answer revision.

**Selected background**:
Candidate-provided experience information chosen to inform a review, such as a resume or personal story; it is not evidence of what was said in that interview.

**Proposed answer**:
Suggested wording the candidate could use in a future interview, based on their interview answer and supported background.
_Avoid_: Corrected transcript, recorded performance.

**Saved revision**:
A proposed future answer the candidate has saved, with or without their own edits; saving it does not establish that they have delivered it in an interview.
_Avoid_: Improved performance.

**Preparation priority**:
An action the candidate chooses to focus on when preparing for a future interview.
_Avoid_: Recurring theme, which implies evidence across interviews.
