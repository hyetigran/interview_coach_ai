# Start with recordings of completed interviews

The first delivery is an invited pilot for software-engineering candidates who provide audio or video recordings of completed hiring or mock interviews, up to 60 minutes. This replaces the text-excerpt-first plan because candidates are expected to bring recordings from tools such as Google Meet or Zoom; requiring them to prepare transcripts would omit the intended intake workflow.

Coaching evaluates spoken answer content, with timestamped transcript evidence and audio playback. Account-connected ingestion, visual/vocal assessment, synchronized video playback, and automatic recurring themes are deferred to keep the pilot focused while transcription and speaker confirmation add scope. Retain the original recording and playback audio until review deletion so candidates can inspect and correct the evidence behind advice.
