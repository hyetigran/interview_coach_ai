# Five-candidate pilot protocol

Status: preparation only. No candidate sessions or pilot outcomes have been recorded here. Ticket #16 remains open and depends on ticket #15. Use this protocol after deployed functional acceptance and the independent quality gate are documented; local test results alone do not authorize a pilot launch.

Recruitment status: the five candidates have not yet been recruited. The current requested deliverable is these preparation materials; sessions and measured outcomes remain pending.

## Before the first session

Record the deployed commit and Worker versions, environment, supported formats/byte limits, measured processing bounds, known limitations, and links to the #15 acceptance evidence and [independent evaluation report](../evaluation/report-template.md). Verify external-provider, Workflow and backup retention, plus deletion behavior, before intake. Record the actual reports, not merely links to blank templates.

Recruit five software-engineering candidates who have permission to supply a hiring interview or externally conducted mock interview. Confirm recording permission privately before intake. Do not ask candidates to paste recordings, transcripts, resumes or permission records into issues. Keep the participant/contact mapping and consent evidence in restricted storage outside Git; assign random participant codes that reveal no identity. Do not contact or invite anyone automatically from this protocol.

Freeze the five-person cohort before observing outcomes. Log withdrawals, failures and incomplete sessions among those five; do not replace them to improve the result. If fewer than five are available, report the pilot as incomplete rather than changing the denominator. Normal speaker confirmation and candidate-led corrections are product use, not facilitator help.

Confirm configuration and ledger state: three recording admissions per candidate, one active automatic job per account, and a shared $50 processing cap covering paid media preparation, transcription, grouping, coaching and retries. Record known settled cost, unresolved reservations and remaining admission headroom separately. Hosting/storage costs do not belong in the $50 processing total. Do not assume fifteen hour-long recordings fit the cap, and do not increase the cap to make the study complete. Earlier paid work charged to this same ledger still consumes the allowance.

## Observe without coaching the participant

Give every candidate the same task: “Use your interview recording to review your answers and save preparation you would find useful for a future interview.” Explain consent and access arrangements before timing. Do not supply step-by-step product instructions, choose the candidate’s voice, repair their answers, or suggest which advice they should call useful.

Observe recording intake, automatic processing, speaker confirmation, question navigation, evidence playback, any corrections, and saved preparation. Allow leaving and returning while processing; record active effort separately from elapsed processing/wait time. A candidate must personally judge at least one saved suggestion useful for the outcome to count. A saved answer alone does not imply usefulness, correctness, or independent completion. Record priorities separately; neither priorities alone nor facilitator-saved work satisfies the saved-suggestion requirement.

If help is requested or needed, record the exact intervention privately, its stage, and whether it changed completion. Support the person as needed, but classify that outcome as assisted. Log product failures, abandoned steps and missing observations explicitly. Never convert “not observed” into zero effort or success. Do not collect passwords, API keys or unnecessary screen recordings.

For each frozen participant, record:

- Recording origin, duration and supported format; intake attempts and admission count.
- Furthest stage reached; independent completion, assisted completion, failure, withdrawal or not observed.
- Candidate usefulness judgment and count of useful saved suggestions; distinguish generated wording from candidate edits.
- Correction count/type and active effort, upload/processing failures, retries and waiting time.
- Known processing cost and unresolved billing by operation category; keep identifiers privately for reconciliation.
- Deletion requests, immediate access denial, cleanup completion/pending status and external retention limits.
- Another recording available: yes, no or unknown; actual return within the predeclared observation window.

Set and record the return observation window before the first session. Report return only among candidates known to have another recording; list unknown eligibility separately. A denominator of zero means “not measurable,” not zero retention. A return is observed use with another recording, not merely saying they intend to return.

## Stop conditions and reporting

Invented personal facts or material ownership misattribution are critical defects. Preserve only the necessary private evidence, suspend the affected generation capability, correct the defect and rerun the applicable independent evaluation before re-enabling it. Record the defect and disposition in the public report without quoting candidate content. Do not average critical defects into a favorable usefulness score. Budget exhaustion or unresolved billing is an observed stopping condition, not permission to bypass the ledger.

Use the [report template](report-template.md). Compute the primary signal with the fixed denominator five: count only candidates who independently completed their review **and** saved at least one suggestion they judged useful. Report the count and whether it reaches four of five; if observations are missing, label the report incomplete. Show assisted and failed outcomes rather than hiding them in a single percentage.

Reconcile costs after sessions. Report settled processing cost separately from unresolved reserved bounds and hosting/storage. Do not describe a reservation as a settled charge or an unresolved charge as free. Include latency ranges, failure counts and correction effort with their sample sizes. Describe this as a small usability signal, separate from independent coaching quality, retention and hiring outcomes.

Before publishing, remove names, contact information, employers, exact session dates, private object/job IDs, recording links, transcript quotes and resume details. Use aggregate findings and random participant codes only where needed to preserve the five outcomes; omit granular combinations that could identify a participant. Keep detailed evidence and the identity mapping private. Publish limitations and the next decision: fix/retest, continue a bounded pilot, or stop. A broader release needs its own evidence and authorization.
