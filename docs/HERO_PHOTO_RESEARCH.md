# Hero photo research

Researched October 1, 2026. The requested direction is a photorealistic image of a potential user. The previous 3D illustration was rejected.

[Visual comparison](hero-photo-references/index.html)

| Reference | Observed photography | Direction for this app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Big Interview](https://www.biginterview.com/) | Hero video showing a woman speaking toward a laptop; the saved reference is its poster frame. | A candidate preparing or practicing an answer, with a relaxed expression. Recommended. |
| [BetterUp](https://www.betterup.com/how-it-works-for-employees) | A close portrait against a plain blue background, with natural skin texture and room for text. | A calm professional portrait in a light neutral setting. |
| [Springboard](https://www.springboard.com/) | The “Make your move in 3 to 9 months” section shows a person taking notes beside a laptop at home. | A candidate reviewing an answer or transcript at a desk. A brighter setting would suit our page. |

## Recommendation

Choose the interview practice direction. It connects the image to the product without adding explanatory copy. Show one adult candidate at a laptop in a light, neutral room. Use natural daylight, visible fabric and skin texture, a relaxed posture, and a small satisfied smile. A muted green shirt or background detail can connect it to the Green/Mist theme.

For the minimalist landing page, use one rectangular foreground photograph, with no UI overlays or badges. Keep the face visible and the CTA clear. Background photography would add more contrast constraints beside the existing carousel.

The source images are saved in `docs/hero-photo-references/` as research references. No new generated photograph or replacement has been applied to the app in this research pass.
